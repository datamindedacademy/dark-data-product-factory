<!--
The capstone runs from Thursday afternoon to Friday, 14:00. Fork the repository: your team owns the whole line, and every station already works. On Thursday the whole team takes one agent, the one where the most points are, and goes through the full loop. Hand out the interview recording now: it holds the company know-how no agent can find in the data.

[click] Write a few benchmark cases for that agent and score it as it is. Without a score you can't tell whether a change helped.

[click] Read the traces of the cases that lost points: what went wrong, and why?

[click] Change one thing in its context: the prompt, a skill with Fernhill's rules, a tool or an MCP server. Run the benchmark again. Better: keep the change. Worse: undo it. Then the next round, until Thursday ends.

[click] On Friday, take the same loop to as many other agents of the line as you want. Pick by where the points are lost, or add an agent of your own. Then freeze your fork; the final runs and everything is done by 14:00.
